The $54,000 Boulderado figure is dead

The earlier one-pager priced the Boulderado at its contracted minimums. That was the wrong thing to plan against. The $28,800 food and beverage minimum was written for a 120 person group, and feeding this agenda at 150 costs roughly $73,650, two and a half times the minimum. A minimum is a floor, not a forecast. The real Boulderado number is $115,000.

Both hotels here are priced at 150 people, which is now our number, so these figures hold as a fallback.

With the dates moved a week earlier, the St Julien has rooms open for a block. Its event space isn't needed with Pearl West.

Where the risk sits

Boulderado, the seventy room per night minimum. Meeting space is contingent on 70 rooms across 3 nights with no stated attrition allowance. Endeavor is not paying for rooms, but if unsold rooms fall back to us that is roughly $77,000 of unbudgeted exposure. This is the largest single unknown across both proposals.

Boulderado, the minimum is not the cost. Any budget built on $28,800 of food and beverage will be short by roughly $45,000 once 150 people are actually fed.

Limelight, AV labor and multi-day rates. Image AV bills technicians at $125 an hour with no discount and states its published prices are estimates. Whether equipment rebills at a reduced rate on days two and three is worth about $8,000.

Limelight, the waiver is conditional. All space is free only if the $80,000 minimum is achieved. A shortfall becomes a charge and may reinstate rental.

Both, 150 is load bearing. Limelight states material changes to attendee or room counts may change pricing, and the Boulderado proposal is written for 120 to 150.
